I'm Mihai Tocitu, AI Product Designer at Ivado Labs.

With 15 years in UX, I specialize in AI-native enterprise UX, translating business logic and data science into a single design language. My work spans demand forecasting, alarm detection, pricing optimization, document drafting, and inventory systems - across supply chain, airlines, retail, logistics, and security, for some of Canada's and the world's largest organizations. Working closely with PMs, engineers, and data scientists, I've delivered results that speak for themselves: 7% increase in airfare buy-up revenue, 10% ancillary revenue growth, and a 30% onboarding increase on a Jira automation platform used by enterprise teams worldwide.

I keep up to date - Google UX Pro and Maven AI-Powered UX certified - and stay hands-on with the AI tooling and models I design around, not just the interfaces on top of them.

I also build Claude skills that help design teams move research, documentation and prototyping to AI-assisted workflows.

Outside of the office, I'm a dad to two humans and three cats, and I grow berries in my backyard with mixed results (OKRs pending).

Happy to take time and deep-dive into anything you see above. Let's talk UX!
